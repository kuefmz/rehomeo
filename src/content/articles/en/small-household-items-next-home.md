Small objects can be useful when they are complete, clean and easy to identify. Kitchen utensils, books, tableware and storage items may be considered individually. A mixed bag described only as “household goods” makes it difficult to assess usefulness or plan handling.

## Plan the details

Group similar objects and note approximate quantities. Pack fragile items securely and label boxes with their contents rather than personal information. Remove documents, medication and anything with sensitive data. Do not assume that electrical products or specialist waste can join a normal household-item collection; ask for a separate assessment when necessary.

## A practical example

A box of kitchenware is easier to evaluate when cups, utensils and fragile plates are grouped and counted. A box containing the same objects mixed with private paperwork requires sorting first. Label contents by category, wrap sharp or breakable edges safely and keep the box manageable rather than filling it beyond practical handling.

Use a short packing list for each category: contents, quantity, condition and any fragile pieces. If a box contains only some parts of a set, say so rather than calling it complete. Keep lids, accessories and instructions with the relevant item. A smaller collection is not automatically simple: mixed contents can take time to sort. Agree whether sorting is your responsibility or part of the requested work, and remove personal material first.

## Your checklist

- Group by item type.
- Record quantity and condition.
- Protect fragile objects.
- Remove private documents.
- Flag electrical or unusual items.

## Sources and limitations

Longer use can reduce waste and demand for replacement products. The outcome depends on condition, transport, storage and actual reuse. We do not calculate or promise carbon savings. The environmental context is general, not a measurement of this service. FOEN explains that the whole life cycle must be considered when assessing outcomes. No per-item or collection-level savings are claimed here. [BAFU / FOEN / OFEV / UFAM](https://www.bafu.admin.ch/en/circular-economy).
