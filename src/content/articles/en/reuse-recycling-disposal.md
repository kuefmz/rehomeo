Reuse preserves the item for further use. Recycling processes materials to make them useful again. Disposal is a route for waste that does not result in the same item continuing in use. Calling all three “recycling” hides decisions customers need to understand.

## Plan the details

Donation and resale are ways of transferring an item, not proof of its environmental outcome. Repair may enable reuse, but suitability and legal treatment of waste still need assessment. Ask which route is proposed for each category and what happens if a planned recipient cannot accept it. Do not interpret a general preference for reuse as a promise that nothing becomes waste.

## A practical example

A table passed on intact is reused. A broken wooden item processed through an appropriate material route is a different case. Selling the table instead of donating it changes the transfer arrangement, not the meaning of reuse. Ask how the provider records and explains each route rather than accepting one broad label for everything.

When comparing providers, ask for understandable categories rather than a slogan. You should be able to see whether an item is intended for further use, material recovery or another agreed waste route. The destination may remain uncertain at enquiry stage; that uncertainty should be stated. If resale is part of the arrangement, clarify it before approval. Neither a donation label nor a recycling label establishes a carbon benefit without looking at the actual process.

## Your checklist

- Separate products from material routes.
- Ask about repair and recipient suitability.
- Distinguish donation from resale.
- Agree handling of unsuitable items.
- Avoid “zero waste” assumptions.

## Sources and limitations

Longer use can reduce waste and demand for replacement products. The outcome depends on condition, transport, storage and actual reuse. We do not calculate or promise carbon savings. The environmental context is general, not a measurement of this service. FOEN explains that the whole life cycle must be considered when assessing outcomes. No per-item or collection-level savings are claimed here. [BAFU / FOEN / OFEV / UFAM](https://www.bafu.admin.ch/en/circular-economy).
