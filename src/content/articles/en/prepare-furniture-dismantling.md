Preparation should preserve usefulness while making collection practical. Empty cupboards, remove private belongings and set aside loose shelves or fixings. Ask whether dismantling is included and who is responsible before taking anything apart.

## Plan the details

For pieces that genuinely need dismantling, retain instructions and label parts so they can be reunited. Put hardware in a secure bag attached to the corresponding item. Photographing assembly details for your own use can help, but no photos are required to submit a request. Electrical or wall-mounted items may need separate qualified work; do not assume collection includes disconnection.

## A practical example

For a bed frame, distinguish the frame from the mattress and ask about each item’s acceptance. Confirm who dismantles the frame and keep bolts with the relevant parts. Do not place small fittings loose in a general box. If the piece is damaged, disclose that before discussing whether dismantling will make collection possible.

Agree the sequence before preparing the room. It may be better for the team to see the assembled item before dismantling, or for you to complete agreed preparation beforehand. Keep clear access to the pieces being collected and avoid blocking shared escape routes. If a part is too heavy or awkward to move safely, leave it in place and describe it. Preparation should reduce uncertainty and preserve the furniture, not create a lifting or assembly problem.

## Your checklist

- Empty drawers and cupboards.
- Confirm dismantling responsibility.
- Keep parts and hardware together.
- Identify fragile components.
- Avoid unsafe lifting or disconnection.

## Sources and limitations

Longer use can reduce waste and demand for replacement products. The outcome depends on condition, transport, storage and actual reuse. We do not calculate or promise carbon savings. The environmental context is general, not a measurement of this service. FOEN explains that the whole life cycle must be considered when assessing outcomes. No per-item or collection-level savings are claimed here. [BAFU / FOEN / OFEV / UFAM](https://www.bafu.admin.ch/en/circular-economy).
